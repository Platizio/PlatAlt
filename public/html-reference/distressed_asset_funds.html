Distressed Asset Funds

Navigating complex value recovery through precision turnaround and strategic capital injection.

The Mechanism

Capitalizing on the Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code (IBC) frameworks to acquire undervalued entities.

Distressed Asset Funds focus on acquiring debt or equity in companies undergoing financial duress. By leveraging legal frameworks and restructuring expertise, these funds aim to unlock latent value that standard market participants overlook.

"True value is often obscured by temporary structural distress. Our role is the surgical restoration of fiscal health."

Structural Complexity

Taxation Framework

As Category II AIFs, these funds enjoy tax pass-through status. Investors are taxed on income streams (Capital Gains or Business Income) as if they held the assets directly, depending on the nature of the recovery.

Risk Profile

These are high-risk instruments. Risks include legal delays in IBC proceedings, failure of turnaround strategies, and illiquidity over the 8-year lock-in period. High-reward potential is balanced by complete capital risk.
