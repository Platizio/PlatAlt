The Core Mission

Angel Funds are a sub-category of Venture Capital Funds that pool investments from "Angel Investors" to back start-ups in their formative years.

Beyond mere capital, these funds represent a commitment to backing new ideas that possess the potential for exponential growth and systemic impact.
