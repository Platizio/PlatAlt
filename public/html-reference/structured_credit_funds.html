Key Characteristics

01

Tranche Structuring

Prioritized cash flows allow for precise risk-return tailoring, from senior secured to equity-like first-loss positions.

02

Mezzanine Logic

Subordinated positioning that bridges the gap between senior debt and equity, typically featuring warrants for equity participation.

03

Convertible Optionality

Instruments that offer the downside protection of credit with the appreciation potential of conversion into underlying equity.
