Private Credit & Yield Strategies

Direct Lending

Providing senior loans directly to mid-market companies, often with floating rates to hedge against inflation.

Mezzanine Debt

Subordinated debt with equity-like kickers, offering higher potential returns for increased risk appetite.

Distressed Credit

Capitalizing on market inefficiencies and corporate restructuring for deep-value opportunities.
